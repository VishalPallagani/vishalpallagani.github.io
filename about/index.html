Vishal Pallagani

I work on environment research at Patronus AI. I build reinforcement-learning environments for computer-use and tool-use agents (enterprise software, diagramming tools, survey tools) and study why models fail in them.

Most of what I care about sits between evaluation and training: when a benchmark measures what it claims, when a reward is wrong, and which model failures are properties of the model rather than of one app.

Before that, I did PhD research at the AI Institute of the University of South Carolina (AIISC) with Biplav Srivastava and Amit Sheth, on language models for automated planning, and was a research intern at IBM Research, where I built Plansformer, a language model that generates valid symbolic plans.

Entries here come in four kinds. A result leads with a number and the evidence for it. Anopinion says what I think and why. work describes something I built. Anote is short.
